# NPO Skills

AI skills for non-profit organisations — fundraising, impact measurement, compliance, and volunteer management. Designed to give under-resourced NPOs the operational capability of a full development office.

The core principle: **NPOs don't lack compelling missions — they lack reach, technology, and capacity.** These skills close that gap.

## Subdomains

| Path | Focus | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| `biz/npo/fundraising/` | Funder discovery, grant writing, donor pipeline, CSI landscape | production |
| `biz/npo/impact/` | Impact reporting, case studies, multiplier calculators | production |
| `biz/npo/compliance/` | PBO registration, Section 18A, grant reporting deadlines | production |
