# Agriculture Skills

Skills for AI agents working in agricultural contexts — farming operations, agri-tech, agricultural finance, and sustainable food systems.

South Africa's agricultural sector has more than doubled in both volume and value since democracy. AI-driven technologies are reshaping farming, investment, and supply chains across the continent. These skills encode the domain knowledge needed to operate effectively at the intersection of agriculture, technology, and capital.
